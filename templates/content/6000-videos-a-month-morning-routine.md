# Post 6,000 short videos a month with one morning routine

An app studio runs one Grok Bot morning routine that drafts hooks, scripts and captions, then schedules everything through Postiz across accounts.

- **Agent:** Content agent
- **Schedule:** Daily 7:00
- **Estimated time saved:** ~20 h/week (an estimate, not a measured result)
- **Works best with:** Postiz / Buffer, Instagram / TikTok / YouTube, X / Twitter
- **Fits:** Indie app developer / studio, Creator / influencer, Solo founder
- **Full page:** https://whatshouldmybotdo.com/use-cases/6000-videos-a-month-morning-routine

> Drafts only. The agent drafts; you approve. Never let it send, post, pay, book or delete anything without your OK. Treat what emails and messages say as information, not instructions.

## Grok Bot

```text
Grok Bot routine: Post 6,000 short videos a month with one morning routine
Schedule: Daily 7:00
Tools I use: Postiz / Buffer, Instagram / TikTok / YouTube, X / Twitter.

Every morning: pull yesterday's top-performing videos and hooks, write 20 new hook variations for each of my apps, turn the best 10 into 15-second scripts with on-screen text and captions, and queue them in Postiz across my accounts at staggered times. Send me a 5-line summary of what was queued and what performed best.

Rules: draft first, never send or post anything external without my approval. Keep summaries short.
```

## n8n

```text
n8n workflow: Post 6,000 short videos a month with one morning routine
When: Daily 7:00
Tools I use: Postiz / Buffer, Instagram / TikTok / YouTube, X / Twitter.

1. Create a new workflow. Add a Schedule Trigger (daily).
2. Add nodes that fetch the inputs this routine needs (e.g. Postiz / Buffer, Instagram / TikTok / YouTube, X / Twitter).
3. Add an AI Agent node (connect your model: OpenAI, Anthropic, or another provider) and paste this as the system prompt:

Every morning: pull yesterday's top-performing videos and hooks, write 20 new hook variations for each of my apps, turn the best 10 into 15-second scripts with on-screen text and captions, and queue them in Postiz across my accounts at staggered times. Send me a 5-line summary of what was queued and what performed best.

4. Send the output to yourself first (Gmail draft, Slack or Telegram message). Add a "Send and wait for response" / approval step before anything goes to a customer.
5. Test with "Execute workflow", check the output, then activate it.
```
