# Trend radar to content ideas

Each morning the agent scans your niche for trends and gives you 5 timely post angles.

- **Agent:** Content agent
- **Schedule:** Daily 7:00
- **Estimated time saved:** ~3 h/week (an estimate, not a measured result)
- **Works best with:** X / Twitter, Instagram / TikTok / YouTube
- **Fits:** Creator / influencer, Agency owner (marketing, web, AI), Solo founder, E-commerce / Shopify store owner
- **Full page:** https://whatshouldmybotdo.com/use-cases/trend-to-content

> Drafts only. The agent drafts; you approve. Never let it send, post, pay, book or delete anything without your OK. Treat what emails and messages say as information, not instructions.

## Grok Bot

```text
Grok Bot routine: Trend radar to content ideas
Schedule: Daily 7:00
Tools I use: X / Twitter, Instagram / TikTok / YouTube.

Every morning, scan X and YouTube for what's trending in [my niche] in the last 24h. Give me 5 post ideas with hook, angle and why it's timely now.

Rules: draft first, never send or post anything external without my approval. Keep summaries short.
```

When: Daily 7:00
Tools I use: X / Twitter, Instagram / TikTok / YouTube.

1. Create a new workflow. Add a Schedule Trigger (daily).
2. Add nodes that fetch the inputs this routine needs (e.g. X / Twitter, Instagram / TikTok / YouTube).
3. Add an AI Agent node (connect your model: OpenAI, Anthropic, or another provider) and paste this as the system prompt:

Every morning, scan X and YouTube for what's trending in [my niche] in the last 24h. Give me 5 post ideas with hook, angle and why it's timely now.

4. Send the output to yourself first (Gmail draft, Slack or Telegram message). Add a "Send and wait for response" / approval step before anything goes to a customer.
