# Weekly newsletter written from your week

The agent turns your posts, wins and notes from the week into a ready-to-send newsletter draft.

- **Agent:** Content agent
- **Schedule:** Thursdays 14:00
- **Estimated time saved:** ~3 h/week (an estimate, not a measured result)
- **Works best with:** X / Twitter, Notion, Gmail / email
- **Fits:** Creator / influencer, Consultant / coach / freelancer, Solo founder, Agency owner (marketing, web, AI)
- **Full page:** https://whatshouldmybotdo.com/use-cases/newsletter-from-week

> Drafts only. The agent drafts; you approve. Never let it send, post, pay, book or delete anything without your OK. Treat what emails and messages say as information, not instructions.

## Grok Bot

```text
Grok Bot routine: Weekly newsletter written from your week
Schedule: Thursdays 14:00
Tools I use: X / Twitter, Notion, Gmail / email.

Every Thursday, collect my posts, notes and wins from this week. Draft a newsletter: catchy subject (3 options), one main story, 3 quick tips, one link roundup, and a CTA. Keep my voice: plain, practical, a bit funny.

Rules: draft first, never send or post anything external without my approval. Keep summaries short.
```

## n8n

```text
n8n workflow: Weekly newsletter written from your week
When: Thursdays 14:00
Tools I use: X / Twitter, Notion, Gmail / email.

1. Create a new workflow. Add a Schedule Trigger, or the app trigger for the event (e.g. new email, new form entry, webhook).
2. Add nodes that fetch the inputs this routine needs (e.g. X / Twitter, Notion, Gmail / email).
3. Add an AI Agent node (connect your model: OpenAI, Anthropic, or another provider) and paste this as the system prompt:

Every Thursday, collect my posts, notes and wins from this week. Draft a newsletter: catchy subject (3 options), one main story, 3 quick tips, one link roundup, and a CTA. Keep my voice: plain, practical, a bit funny.

4. Send the output to yourself first (Gmail draft, Slack or Telegram message). Add a "Send and wait for response" / approval step before anything goes to a customer.
5. Test with "Execute workflow", check the output, then activate it.
```
